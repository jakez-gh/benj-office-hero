# Office Hero Documentation

Office Hero is a field service management platform built for small service companies — plumbing, HVAC, pest control, landscaping, and more. It brings your jobs, dispatching, vehicles, customers, and technicians into one place so your team can stop juggling spreadsheets and start serving customers faster.

## What Office Hero Does

Office Hero connects every part of your daily operation:

- **Jobs** move through a clear lifecycle — created, dispatched, in progress, complete — and every status change is visible to your whole team in real time.
- **Dispatch** evaluates your technicians and generates optimized route options in under 8 seconds so you can get the right person to the right job without guesswork.
- **Routes** give each technician a sequenced list of stops for the day. They mark arrivals and completions from their phone; you watch it happen on the map.
- **Vehicles and crews** are paired at the start of each day, making it simple to track who is in what truck and what they can carry.
- **Customers and locations** are stored once. A single customer can have multiple service addresses — a homeowner with a rental property, a restaurant chain with three locations.
- **Contracts** automate recurring work. Set a maintenance agreement to quarterly and Office Hero creates the jobs automatically, so nothing falls through the cracks.
- **Integrations** connect Office Hero to ServiceTitan, PestPac, and Jobber so your back-office data stays in sync without manual entry.

Your account's data is fully isolated from all other accounts and protected by encrypted sessions and HTTPS on every connection.

## Who Uses Office Hero

| Role | What they do in Office Hero |
|---|---|
| **Company Admin** | Full access — settings, users, vehicles, reports, billing |
| **Dispatcher** | Create jobs, dispatch routes, monitor technician progress |
| **Sales** | Create customers, locations, and contracts; view job history |
| **Technician** | View daily route, mark stops, log job notes from the mobile app |
| **Technician Helper** | View assigned stops; limited to read-only on job details |

## Quick-Start Checklist for New Company Admins

Complete these steps to go from a fresh account to your first dispatched job. Each step links to the detailed guide below.

1. [ ] Log in and change your password (see [Getting Started](getting-started.md))
2. [ ] Invite at least one Dispatcher or Technician
3. [ ] Add your first vehicle
4. [ ] Assign a technician to the vehicle for today
5. [ ] Add your first customer and a service address
6. [ ] Create a job for that customer
7. [ ] Dispatch the job and confirm the technician received it

Most teams complete this checklist in under 30 minutes. The [Getting Started guide](getting-started.md) walks through every step in detail.
