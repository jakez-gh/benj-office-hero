# Technician Field Guide

Welcome to Office Hero. This guide covers everything you need to get through your day — from logging in to completing your last stop. If you run into trouble at any point, reach out to your dispatcher or contact us at <support@officehero.dev>, through the in-app chat, or by calling 1-800-HERO-FSM.

---

## Logging In

Office Hero is available two ways: as a native Android app and as a mobile web app you can open in any browser.

### Android App

1. Open the **Office Hero** app on your Android phone.
2. Enter your work email address and your password, then tap **Sign In**.
3. If your company uses single sign-on (SSO), tap **Sign in with your company account** instead and follow the prompts.
4. Once signed in, you land on your **Today's Route** screen automatically.

Your session is encrypted and secured with a time-limited token. You will be asked to sign in again after a period of inactivity — this is a security feature that protects your account and your customers' information.

### Mobile Web Browser

1. Open Chrome, Firefox, or Safari on your phone.
2. Go to **app.officehero.dev** and bookmark the page so you can find it quickly each day.
3. Enter your work email and password, then tap **Sign In**.
4. If you are prompted to "Add to Home Screen," accept — this makes the web app behave almost identically to the native app.

> **Tip:** Use the same credentials for both the Android app and the browser version. Your route and job status sync in real time between them.

## Viewing Today's Route

After signing in, the **Today's Route** screen shows every stop assigned to you for the day.

Each stop card displays:

- The customer name and address
- Your scheduled arrival window
- Your estimated arrival time (ETA), updated automatically as you move through the day
- A color-coded status badge: **Upcoming**, **En Route**, **Arrived**, or **Complete**

Tap any stop card to see full job details — notes from the dispatcher, customer contact information, and the service to be performed.

---

## Understanding Stop Order and ETAs

Your dispatcher sets the sequence of your stops each morning (or the night before). The order you see in the app is the order you should follow unless your dispatcher tells you otherwise.

ETAs are calculated automatically. The system looks at your current location, the distance to each upcoming stop, and typical travel times to give customers an accurate arrival estimate. ETAs refresh every time your location updates — which happens every **30 seconds** while the app is open.

If a job runs long and you fall behind, ETAs for your remaining stops adjust automatically. You do not need to do anything — your dispatcher can already see the updated times and will contact affected customers if needed.

## Marking "Arrived" at a Stop

When you pull up to a job site:

1. Tap the stop card for the current job.
2. Tap **I've Arrived**.
3. The stop status changes to **Arrived** and the arrival time is logged.

This timestamp is used for payroll records and customer communication, so mark it as soon as you are on site — not after you have gathered your tools.

---

## Marking a Stop "Complete"

Before you tap Complete, make sure you have filled in all required fields. Rushing through this step can delay invoicing.

1. From the job detail screen, tap **Complete Job**.
2. Fill in or confirm the following (required fields are marked with an asterisk):
   - **Work performed** — a brief description of what was done
   - **Materials used** — list any parts or materials, with quantities
   - **Customer signature** — have the customer sign on your phone screen (tap **Request Signature**)
   - **Photos** — tap **Add Photo** to attach before-and-after images if your dispatcher has requested them
3. Tap **Submit** to mark the job complete.

The dispatcher and your back office are notified immediately. The customer may receive an automatic completion summary depending on your company's settings.

## Skipping a Stop

Sometimes a stop cannot be completed — the customer is not home, access is blocked, or the job needs to be rescheduled. Only skip a stop if you cannot complete it and your dispatcher has confirmed the skip.

1. From the job detail screen, tap **Skip Stop**.
2. Select a reason from the dropdown (for example: "Customer not home," "Access blocked," "Rescheduled by customer").
3. Add any extra notes in the text field — be specific, because this goes into the customer record.
4. Tap **Confirm Skip**.

The stop is removed from your active route for today. Your dispatcher receives an alert and will handle rebooking or customer communication.

> **Note:** You cannot skip a stop without selecting a reason. The reason field is required.

## How GPS Works

When the app is open, Office Hero shares your location with your dispatcher every **30 seconds**. This means:

- Your ETA calculations stay accurate throughout the day — customers and dispatchers always see a realistic arrival window, not a stale estimate from this morning.
- Your dispatcher can see your position on a live map. If an urgent job comes in near you, they can assign it without having to call first.
- It acts as a safety check-in. If something happens on the road, your company knows your last known location.

**Your location is only shared while the app is open and you are signed in.** It stops when you sign out or close the app. Your location data is visible only to your dispatcher and company administrator — it is never shared with customers or external parties.

If you see a banner asking for location permission, tap **Allow**. Without location access, ETAs will not update and your dispatcher will not be able to see you on the map.

## What to Do When Something Goes Wrong

### No Route Showing Today

If you sign in and see an empty route screen:

1. Pull down to refresh the screen and wait a few seconds.
2. Confirm you are signed in to the correct account — check that your name appears in the top-right corner.
3. Contact your dispatcher directly. Your route may not have been assigned yet, or you may have been accidentally left off today's schedule.
4. If your dispatcher confirms the route was sent and you still see nothing, contact support at <support@officehero.dev> or 1-800-HERO-FSM.

### Can't Mark Complete — Job Already Marked by Someone Else

If you tap Complete and see a message saying the job is already marked, another team member (or your dispatcher) completed it on your behalf. Check with your dispatcher before taking any further action — the job may already be closed out in the system.

### Can't Mark Complete — Network Issue

If you lose signal mid-job:

1. The app will save your inputs locally and try to sync as soon as your connection returns.
2. Do not close the app — keep it open in the background.
3. Once you see the status change to **Complete** with a checkmark, the sync was successful.
4. If you are in an area with no coverage for an extended period, call your dispatcher to let them know so they can update the job manually.

### App Crashes or Won't Load

1. Force-close the app and reopen it.
2. If it still will not load, check your internet connection — switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to see if one works better.
3. Try the mobile web version at **app.officehero.dev** as a backup. Your route and job data are identical.
4. If neither works, call your dispatcher and contact support at 1-800-HERO-FSM.

### Wrong Address Showing

Do not navigate to an incorrect address. Instead:

1. Call your dispatcher immediately.
2. Tap the three-dot menu on the stop card and select **Report Address Issue**.
3. Your dispatcher can correct the address and push the update to your app — the stop card will refresh within seconds once corrected.
